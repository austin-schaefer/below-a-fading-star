# Roadmap

Not a schedule. What is being built now, what the owner has asked for next (if anything), and the pool of their open ideas. Future milestones are not planned in advance: whoever opens the next milestone chooses it then, from this pool, from what the walk needs, and from their own ideas (`autonomous-run` skill, "Choosing what to build"). The owner pins one by saying "this one next" in an issue or a session; nothing else sets an order.

Labels: a kind (`milestone`, `bug`, `tweak`, `harness`), `agent` on anything an agent filed, and a state (`in-progress` with its version, `next`, or `idea`). An issue is closed by the release that ships it, with the release linked.
