# v17, for every agent

Read `AGENTS.md`, then load `wilderness` and `world-code` (with `runtime.md` and `modules.md`), `visual-art` (with `land.md`, and `architecture.md` for anything built) for anything a visitor sees, and `storytelling` if your brief points at a story file. Read `plans/wilderness-v17.md` (short; its survey has the numbers) and `briefs/v17/index.md` (the plan's coordinates and levels, who owns what).

Rules for this milestone:

- **The machine is the owner's and it is in use.** One headless browser at a time, renders and tests in sequence; `nice -n 10` in front of every `node tools/...`; no `--trace`. Serve your own worktree on your own port and set `FP_BASE=http://localhost:<your port>` for every tool that loads the page (`tools/views.mjs` and others default to 8123, the working branch's server: the planner rendered against it by mistake). Never serve, check out or edit anything in `.claude/worktrees/deploy`. Kill your server and any browser when you hand back.
- **Waiting on a run:** never a `pgrep -f` loop (`wilderness`, "Waiting on a run"). Wait on a process id or a line in a log, and never leave a waiter behind.
- **One plan file.** Every coordinate and level of this milestone lives in `src/v17/plan.js` (three-free; imports only `world.js`), which W1 writes in its first push from the index's table. Read your numbers from it; add your own rows to it in your own section; never change another brief's rows without saying so in your report (a level that moves moves for everyone downstream: the mere's level is the Swallow's sill). New code goes in new files under `src/v17/`.
- **Others are at work beside you** (the index says who owns what). Keep edits to `src/main.js`, `src/world.js`, `src/settings.js`, `src/controls.js`, `src/post.js`, `src/height/field.js`, `src/height/floor.js`, `src/water.js`, `src/water/river.js`, the audio graph and the skill references to the lines your brief names.
- **The height path is already 8% slower over the valley than v15's.** Nothing you add may cost a call outside your box: every new term in `heightAt`, `biomeAt` and `waterLevelAt` sits behind one box test (the chain's box is x -125..25, z 195..335), the way `SMALL_BOX` guards the floor's pools. Measure with `nice -n 10 node tools/height-speed.mjs --base=origin/next --sets=valley,fen` twice, before you hand back; a ratio over 1.02 on `valley` is a fault.
- **Moving the ground moves what stands on it.** Any change to `heightAt`, `biomeAt`, a region's weights or what scatter reads runs `threads`, `seams`, `geometry`, `roads`, `paths`, `outline`, `regions`, `rocks`, `dressing`, `strata` (`ONLY=... tools/suite.sh <port>`) and `walkers` (`nice -n 10 node tools/walkers-test.mjs $B`, not in the suite: 19/20 on v16, P12's pit-lamps red on `main` too). v16's floor left a shrine unbuilt, a path's end under water and a tree in a doorway, found only by the batch's suite. `seams` and `geometry`: list the rows you would re-record and why; do not re-record wholesale.
- **Judged moving.** The owner on water: "a lot of water looks very janky". Anything that moves, or that a visitor does (water running, water at a shore, a walk into a tube, a light that breathes), is looked at in frames taken in order with your report shut (`tools/step-frames.mjs`, or `views.mjs` with a `pre` that advances the clock), at medium and low. A still of the end state does not count. A blind reader (a helper on `sonnet`, shown only the frames, asked "what is this, and does anything look wrong?") sees each round before you hand back.
- **A fault is fixed at its cause, and its kind is guarded:** add a row to an existing test where one fits (`outline` for water, `edges` and `under-geometry` for under the ground, `heights` for a way up or down).
- **Names.** A place a visitor meets is named in `LANDMARKS` only by the brief that says so, with its `line` empty: an agent never writes a quotation (the owner gives them). Everything else stays an identifier and a comment.
- **Phones.** Render the low tier; stay inside the module budgets (`world-code`). Nothing in this milestone may make the low tier slower; under the ground, the seal (`below.keep`, `LAYER_BELOW`) is what keeps a room cheap.
- **Tests before handing back:** the ones your change touches and any you add, by name: `ONLY=name,name tools/suite.sh <port>` (say the counts; zero tests is a failure). Never the whole suite on a branch.
- **Renders** go in `shots/wip/` as `v17-<brief>-<stage>-<view>.png`. The fixed views are `tools/views-v17-water.json`; the before set is `shots/wip/v17-before-*.png` in the deploy worktree (read it there; never write there). Render the views your brief names from that list after every round. Look at every frame before you name it; never delete one.
- **Helpers run small.** Frames handed to helper agents go to `haiku` or `sonnet`, never your own model.
- Document in `world-code` (`runtime.md` or `modules.md`), present tense, a few lines, what the next agent needs; keep the story file you build from true to what is built (its State, Beats and Log).
- Commit and push after every part (never `--no-verify`, never force). Write your report into `briefs/v17/reports/<branch>.md` and push it. Hand back as `world-code` says, in under 250 words: what changed, the commands and counts, the frames to look at in order (paths), what is still weak, any default you took, and a `?pos=` link the owner can open on the working branch's server (8123) to see it.
