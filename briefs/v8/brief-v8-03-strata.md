# Brief v8-03: three hands

**Goal.** A visitor can tell who built a thing by looking at it, sees three different builders, and sees each one using what the last one left. The owner: "architectural cues that reoccur", and "the goal is still diversity, but with coherence. i like that the architecture today has both cyclopean bridges and arabesque tavernas." So this brief does not unify the styles. It makes each stratum more itself, gives each its rendering of one sign, and plants each stratum's leavings in the next one's work. Read the bible in `briefs/v8/index.md` closely, including the audit paragraph.

**Build.**
1. **`src/v8/strata.js`** (and `src/v8/strata/`), on `ctx.strata`: the three hands as a small kit other v8 briefs call. For each stratum: its material(s), its rendering of the sign as geometry a caller can place (Elder: a notch cut 3–8 m; Heliarch: a carved roundel in a notched head, 0.4–1.2 m, bronze or ember-glass disc; Late: a painted or tiled panel and a cut-out finial, the notch gone crescent, the disc a pomegranate), and two or three signature parts (Elder: a triangle-etched block; Heliarch: a hexagonal column drum, a capital, a bronze-bound slab; Late: a plastered wall panel with a horseshoe or ogee opening, a pierced screen, a coloured lamp on a bracket, a striped awning, a tiled bench). Keep the API small and documented at the top of the file; briefs 05–08 will use it.
2. **The Late folk get their colour.** Re-skin the Pomegranate House (`src/structures/taverna.js`) from grey stone to lime plaster in warm colours with tile bands and painted woodwork, keeping its plan and arches; give the Oast Byre and Medlar Close's and the Lynchets' walls and gate smaller touches of the same hand (a painted door, a tiled niche, a rag awning, limewash). The house is brief 05's to light and people; you give it its walls and you leave brackets where lamps will hang. In the render from 60 m it must be the one building on the map nobody would mistake for a ruin.
3. **The sign, placed.** Elder notch: on the Span's gates and the Plinth Road's crown, big enough to read from the causeway. Heliarch roundel: over the keep's gate, the tower's door, the Hypogeum's lintel, the court's stair head, the shrine, the Leaning Yard's gate. Late: on the house's alfiz, the byre door, the Lynchets' gate. At least a dozen placements across the map, at least three per stratum.
4. **Spolia.** At least ten placements where one stratum reuses another, each visible from a track or the causeway (01 is extending the tracks; the lead list is in `src/v4/paths/routes.js`): etched Elder blocks as the house's corner and the byre's threshold; Heliarch column drums as the Lynchets' gateposts and a mounting block; a hex paver as a tabletop; a bronze-bound door as a bridge or a bench; a fallen plinth quarried half away with Late wedges still in it; Heliarch ashlar courses raised directly on an Elder footing at the keep or the court. Each one small, deliberate and legible.
5. **`tools/strata-test.mjs`** in the suite: the kit's parts build; counts of sign placements per stratum; each placement stands on the ground (not floating or buried) and within sight of a track or the causeway.

**Read.** `src/v4/kit/index.js` (masonry opts), `src/v4/span/etch.js` (a material can be etched once), `src/structures/materials.js`, `src/structures/geo.js`, `src/structures/taverna.js`, `byre.js`, `scatter.js` (shrine ~253, yard ~294), `src/v2/ruins/hypogeum.js`, `src/v7/glyphs/engrave.js`, `src/v4/regions/lynchets.js` (~208).

**Not yours.** Gnomons (01 builds the waystones, in the Heliarch hand, with its own carving of the roundel; that two carvers differ slightly is intended). Ground and light (02 is making the house's surroundings the warmest place on the map). Story props (04).

**Done when** strata-test passes; before/after pairs of the house from 60 m and 15 m, and one render per sign placement and per spolia placement, are in `shots/wip/`; a sheet of three renders (one building per stratum, same framing) shows three plainly different hands with the same sign on each.
