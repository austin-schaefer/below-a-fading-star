**#11, "Lamplighter walks thru a giant rock"** (no text; the screenshot did not upload). The Lamplighter is the Leerie, who walks the row of twelve lamp standards beside the kerb K1 near (128, 5) (`src/v7/walkers/leerie.js`, `src/v5/lights.js`).

**What they are.** The boulders built in `src/rocks.js`: dark stone with large pale patches (lichen or snow) at high contrast. They are in most of the orchestrator's renders, e.g. `shots/wip/m8-walk-b1-03-causeway-west.png` (right foreground and far right) and `m8-walk-b1-24-moor-open.png` in the deploy worktree's `shots/wip/` (`/Users/austin/dev/below-a-fading-star/.claude/worktrees/deploy/shots/wip/`); render your own befores first and confirm which mesh and which material term makes the spots.

**Build.**
1. **The look.** The pale patches go from spots to something a rock has: a thin grey-green lichen bloom at low contrast, mostly on the tops and the side away from the sun, breaking up at small scale; no white. From 100 m a boulder is a dark quiet shape; from 400 m it is not noticeable at all. The rocks are a supporting player: after this they should never be the thing the eye goes to in a frame. Keep each region's stone its own (granite tors on the High Ling, erratics on the Outwash, black flint on the Strandline: check that the fix does not flatten those; the spots may be one shared term).
2. **How many.** Cut the big ones hard: at a guess to a third of today's count on the moor and common, clustered (a few groups with bare ground between, where a group reads as an outcrop) rather than sprinkled evenly. Small stones and pebbles can stay where they give the ground texture. Brief 02 made the common round the keep a pale grazed lawn: boulders there especially should be few. Report the counts before and after per size class.
3. **Where they must not be.** Nothing bigger than a pebble inside a built place or on anything walked: the two graveyards (the Leaning Yard, `LANDMARKS.graveyard`; the Sexton's graves, `sextonClear` exists), the Pomegranate House and its approach, the Collation, the shrine, the court, the keep's apron, the Demesne lawn, on any road (brief 09 keeps boulders off roads by their whole radius at high: make that hold at every tier), and on the path of anything that walks: the Leerie's row along all twelve standards with 2 m to spare, the Thurifer on P3, the Link-Boy's leads, the grazers' homes. The pattern is the `xClear(x, z)` functions `rocks.js` already imports (`plinthsClear`, `naveClear`, `sextonClear`, ~lines 6–9 and 177–194), tested after the draws so no placement stream moves; add a single table of cleared discs and strips in one place rather than a dozen imports.
4. **A test** so it stays true: extend an existing one or add `tools/rocks-test.mjs` to the suite: no boulder's footprint overlaps the Leerie's walked line, the Thurifer's track, any road sample, or the listed built places; the boulder count on the common is under a stated ceiling; the patch contrast of the boulder material (sample the shader's two colours, or render one boulder on a grey card) is under a stated ratio.

**Constraints.** Fewer rocks changes colliders: `walk-test`, `roads-test`, `walkers-test`, `threads-test`, `twk-test` must stay green. A hop brief (v8-10, in flight in another worktree) is giving rocks true heights so they can be stood on: it edits `src/rocks.js` collider registration; merge `origin/next` before you start and again before you push, and keep your change to placement, clearance and material.

**Done when** the tests pass and before/after pairs from the same poses show: the causeway west of the keep, the open moor, the Leaning Yard, the Leerie's row end to end, and one frame per region with its own stone.
