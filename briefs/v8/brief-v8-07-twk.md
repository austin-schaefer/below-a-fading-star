# Brief v8-07: Twk-men

**Goal.** Something small, quick and funny that a visitor meets in the first few minutes and keeps meeting. The owner: "no cuteness or humor to give the dark things punch". The walk already has them and nobody knows: the Salt-Pedlars (`src/v2/riverlife.js` ~43) are 3.5 cm riders on dragonflies by the river. In Vance they are Twk-men: thumb-high, green-skinned, mounted on dragonflies, sharp traders who sell news and want salt for it, vain, easily insulted. Make them a presence across the map, readable at walking distance, with manners.

**Build**, in `src/v8/twk.js` and `src/v8/twk/` (world module `twk`; `?twk=0`), reusing or replacing the pedlars' model as you judge (if you replace it, the pedlars at the river become part of this module and keep their place and their `riverlife` tests' expectations, or you update the tests and say so):
1. **Scale that reads.** A 3.5 cm rider cannot be seen. Vance's are thumb-sized on large dragonflies: make the dragonfly 25–35 cm across the wings and the rider 6–8 cm, with a silhouette that reads at 15 m against bright sky and against dark ground (pale wings that catch the low sun, a spot of colour on the rider: a red cap, a saffron pennon on a lance). Wings blur; flight is darting, with dead-still hovers.
2. **Where.** Stations: on gnomons (brief 01 puts waystones along every track; find them at run time through `ctx` (01 will say how in `modules.md`), and fall back to points on the leads from `src/v4/paths/routes.js` if none is registered), at the Pomegranate House's gate, at the river by the old pedlars' place, at the Kept Fire. Ten to fourteen riders on the map, two or three in view at most.
3. **Manners**, each one seen without being told, at least four of:
   - **The inspection.** When you come within 12 m of a station a rider flies out, stops dead a metre from your face at eye height, looks you up and down (the dragonfly tilts), and leaves as if unimpressed. Once per station per visit.
   - **The escort.** One flies ahead of you along the track for 30–40 m, looking back, then tires of you.
   - **The quarrel.** Two meet in the air over something and circle each other tightly, then split. Heard as two thin angry voices.
   - **The salute.** They rise and hover in a rank when a Heliarch officer passes (the Thurifer on P3 is the easy one: `src/v7/walkers/thurifer.js`), then drop the moment his back is turned.
   - **Salt** (thread 6, `briefs/v8/index.md`): a split sack on the Salt-Pedlars' road between the Saltings and the house with a spill of white salt on the track (pick a place on a lead or the causeway, within 3 m of it); a crowd of riders ferrying grains away in a line, who all stop and watch you until you pass. The other end of the thread: a neat pile of the same salt at their station at the house, with a rider on guard who will not be moved.
   - **The gutter.** When the sun gutters (`ctx.sky.flicker`) every rider in view lands and holds on; they take off again a beat late.
4. **A voice.** Load the `sound-design` skill and follow it. A wing buzz that is pleasant (a soft filtered flutter, not a raw saw; the craft rules and the roughness gate apply), a thin chirrup of speech for the inspection and the quarrel, pitched in D with the rest. Short, quiet, near. Through the existing voice system (`src/audio/living.js` is the pattern).

**Constraints.** One instanced mesh for dragonflies and one for riders (wing blur in the vertex shader or as a second alpha card), a handful of draw calls; flight on the CPU for the few that are near and held still or on a cheap loop beyond 60 m (`ctx.registerUpdate(fn, { at, near, hz })`). No per-frame allocation. They must never block the view for more than a second or sit in the walker's face while moving: the inspection keeps its metre. Deterministic stations from `ctx.makeRng`; behaviour may depend on the walker. Low tier: fewer riders, same manners.

**`tools/twk-test.mjs`** in the suite: stations exist and are on gnomons or leads; an inspection triggers at 12 m, holds its distance (never under 0.8 m from the camera) and ends; riders land in a gutter; the salt spill and the pile exist within 3 m and 15 m of a track; draw calls within budget.

**Done when** twk-test passes; renders show a rider on a gnomon at 15 m (readable without zoom), the inspection (the rider's face-on hover filling perhaps a twentieth of the frame), the quarrel, the salt line, and the guard at the house; and at least one of them is funny to someone who has not read this brief.
