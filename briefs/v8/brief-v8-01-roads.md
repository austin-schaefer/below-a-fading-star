# Brief v8-01: roads that go somewhere

**Goal.** Standing at the spawn a first-time visitor can see where to walk, and every road they take arrives somewhere and goes on. Today the leads (P1–P17 in `src/v4/paths/routes.js`) are 15–156 m fragments, 0.7–1.1 m wide, in colours within a shade of the turf (`MAT_LOOK` in `src/v4/paths.js`: moor `#3a3024`), and only four of them touch the causeway. Seventeen landmarks have no track within 15 m of their centre: the tarn, fen and wood are reached only at their edge, and the Hawser, the Plinth Road, the Cutting and the Galleries' foot by nothing.

**Build.**
1. **The network.** One connected graph: the causeway is the trunk; every `LANDMARKS` entry is on it or on a track that reaches it; dead ends are joined to the next track or to a place (P4's west end, P5→the court's stair, P7→the Lattice and the Plinth Road, P10's branches, P11→the Nave's rim, P14→P13, P16 through the wood to the Thrones' card and back to the causeway's west end). Add loops, not only spurs: a visitor should be able to walk out one way and home another. Append new leads at the end of `LEADS` (the wander seed is the index, so inserting moves every later lead). Extending an existing lead's `pts` is fine. A track through the fen may be a line of stepping slabs or a plank walk; over the river, a ford or a clapper bridge (Late folk's work, of Heliarch slabs).
2. **Tracks you can see.** A road hierarchy by stratum: the causeway (Heliarch, paved, as now); **ways** (2 m, trodden pale, edged here and there with a kerbstone, the main branches to the big places); **trods** (1 m, the rest). Pale against their ground in every region, most of all in the wood, fen and strandline where today's colour vanishes: aim for a track that reads as a pale line at 80 m in a 1280×720 render from eye height. Cover is worn wider than the tread (a mown verge feel: short or no grass 1–1.5 m each side) so the line is not hidden by blades from a walker's eye. Check whether reeds and fleshy leaves respect `wornAt`; make them.
3. **Gnomons.** The Heliarchy's waystones: a slim stone pillar 2.4–3 m tall, leaning a few degrees toward the sun, its head notched with the disc in it (the sign, Heliarch hand, `briefs/v8/index.md`); the disc a bead of ember glass that catches the sun (not a light source; a faint emissive tied to `ctx.sky.flicker` is welcome). One at every junction (with a low arm or a notch cut toward each branch) and along every way and trod so that from any gnomon the next is in sight (40–70 m apart, closer in the wood and where the ground hides them). Many are fallen, snapped or sunk; a fallen one still marks the way. They cast shadows. One instanced mesh or two, with colliders. The causeway's existing waymarker pillars (`src/structures/causeway.js`, every 40 m) become gnomons of a grander kind or gain the notched head: one family.
4. **The first minute.** From the spawn (`SPAWN` in `src/world.js`, facing west) the frame holds the causeway, at least one way leaving it and at least two gnomons. Move the spawn a few metres or turn it if that makes a better first picture; say so.
5. **`tools/roads-test.mjs`** in the suite: the graph is connected from the spawn; every landmark with a footprint has a track within its radius or 15 m of its centre (list any you exempt and why: the Galleries and Thrones hang on the wall, their cards' triggers are the target); along every track, sampled each 4 m, a gnomon stands within 80 m with clear line of sight over the terrain; every track sample is walkable (slope, not under water unless it is a ford or slabs). Update `paths-test` (it pins 17 paths, ≤ 11 draw calls, < 150k triangles for the leads: keep the budget honest, raise it only with a measured reason), `walkers-test` (≤ 3 live light sources along every path; the Link-Boy runs on P-leads near z 215 and treats P4 specially) and the `AVOID` list as needed.

**Read.** `src/v4/paths.js`, `src/v4/paths/routes.js`, `src/v4/paths/ground.js`, `src/structures/causeway.js`, `src/vegetation/plants.js` (~633–660, `wornAt`), `src/vegetation/ground.js` (~131–145), `tools/paths-test.mjs`, `tools/map.mjs` (render the map before and after: `shots/wip/v8-01-map-before.png`, `-after.png`).

**Not yours.** Region cover and colour (02), buildings (03), story props (04). 02 is changing grass density and ground colour by region at the same time: your tracks must read against paler and barer ground too, so set contrast relative to the ground under them, not as fixed hex.

**Done when** the map shows one joined network; roads-test and the updated paths-test and walkers-test pass; renders from the spawn, from four junctions and from inside the wood, the fen and the Lattice each show the track ahead and the next gnomon without being told where to look.
