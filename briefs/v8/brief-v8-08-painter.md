# Brief v8-08: the painter

**Goal.** Thread 5 of `briefs/v8/index.md`: the Unfinished Prospect (`src/v5/dressing/prospect.js`; an easel, stool, table of pigments and a baked canvas of the view at 238,-30 on the High Ling's brow) stops being a lone prop and becomes the last of a painter's attempts. A visitor walking the road toward it meets worse and worse canvases abandoned at earlier viewpoints, understands that somebody has been trying to paint this country and failing, and at the far end finds the painter, who has given up in a way that is funny. Vance's tone: vanity, exactness, defeat borne with poor grace.

**Build**, as `src/v8/threads/painter.js` in the existing `threads` module (`src/v8/threads.js`; read how `chair.js` and `gold.js` there are built, flagged and tested; `?painter=0`):
1. **Three or four abandoned attempts** along tracks leading to the Prospect (the roads are `ctx.roads` and the leads in `src/v4/paths/routes.js`; P10, P22 and the way from the Hypogeum reach the Prospect; pick viewpoints 60–150 m apart where a painter would stop, each within 5 m of a track, each facing something worth painting: the Span, the keep, the sun in the saddle, the Lattice). Each is a canvas on a makeshift support (propped on stones, wedged in a gnomon's notch, on a broken easel) and each fails differently and legibly at 8 m: one slashed corner to corner; one where the sun has been painted, scraped out and repainted five times, each lower than the last, as it sank while he worked; one abandoned after a single confident line; one kicked through. The paintings are made with `CanvasTexture` drawn in code at build time (no network images; `prospect.js` shows how the existing canvas is mapped), small (256 px), and crude on purpose: a few flat shapes of the view from that spot in the walk's own palette. Dropped things between them: a squeezed tube, a brush snapped in two, a rag.
2. **The painter**, found last, somewhere within sight of the Prospect's easel or of the Pomegranate House (your call; the house is being lit and peopled by brief 05 in `src/v8/house*`, so if you choose the house keep him outside the gate, and say where): alive, a Late-folk figure (patched colour; smock stiff with paint in every colour of the abandoned canvases so the match is instant), who has given up: asleep against his pack with a canvas over his face, or painting a small bright picture of something easy (a medlar, his own boot) with enormous care, his back turned squarely on the view. One small behaviour when you come within 6 m (he turns the little canvas away so you cannot see it; or pulls the canvas further over his face). `Painter` in `src/fauna/uncanny/kit.js` and the workers' rig (`src/v7/workers/rig.js`, `body.js`) are the cheapest way to a figure; `src/v8/livery.js` (brief 06, in flight) will hold the Late folk's colours: if it is on `next` when you merge, use it, else match the bible.
3. **The Prospect itself** gains one touch that ties it in: the same smock's rag on the stool, and the palette's colours matching the tubes dropped on the road.

Clearances for the cover round each site (`excluded()` in `src/vegetation/ground.js`; the pattern in `chair.js`), colliders only where a walker would trip, one or two merged meshes for all the props plus the figure. Settings-menu teleports for the first attempt and the painter, hidden when the thread did not build (04's fix round is adding that mechanism to `src/settings.js`; merge `origin/next` before you touch it).

**Tests.** Extend `tools/threads-test.mjs`: each attempt and the painter exist, stand on their ground, are within 5 m (attempts) or 15 m (painter) of a track, are visible (not `visible === false`, not zero-size), and each attempt's stand point has a clear line to it. `dressing-test` stays green.

**Done when** threads-test passes and there are two renders per attempt (from the track at 8–12 m, and close enough to read the painting) and three of the painter (approach, close, his behaviour), each of which a stranger could caption.
