# Brief v8-04: stories with two ends

**Goal.** A visitor finds something in one place that makes them wonder, and finds the answer somewhere else. The owner: "Stories that start one place and end another: a joke-y example would be a cloak and rucksack on the tall bridge, a splayed body below. The unfortunate traveller met their demise..." Today the three story props (the Slipper on the causeway, the Collation's table, the Unfinished Prospect) each end where they start. You build threads 1, 2 and 4 from `briefs/v8/index.md`. The tone is Vance's: dry, exact, a little cruel, funny. Nothing is explained in words; a thread is told by objects, where they are and what state they are in.

**Build**, in `src/v8/threads.js` and `src/v8/threads/<thread>.js` (world module `threads`; `?threads=0`, and a flag per thread):
1. **The traveller on the Span.** On the deck at the broken bay's edge (`ctx.span.deckAt`, `src/v4/span/plan.js`): boots set neatly side by side, a folded cloak, a rucksack with its flap open and a few things laid out as if chosen (a cup, a book, a coil of rope not long enough). Below, on the ground or in the water under that bay: what is left of him, splayed, in the same cloth so the two ends match at a glance (bones in good clothes; old, not gory), and round him the things a fall scatters. A third beat: his hat, somewhere a hat would blow to, on a track between the two ends. Find how a walker gets up to the deck (the two stairs) and make sure both ends can be reached and each is seen from the way to the other: from the deck you can look down and find him; from him you can look up and see the broken bay.
2. **The twelfth chair.** The Collation (`src/v5/dressing/collation.js`, table at 141,-66, twelve places, one chair knocked over today) loses a chair: a gap at the table with its place still laid and drag marks leaving the clearing. The chair stands at the head of the grave the Sexton is digging (`src/v7/workers/sexton.js`, `graves.js`; the open one is the forty-first), with the missing place's cup and plate on its seat and the Sexton's coat over its back. Between the two, along the road, two or three marks of a chair being carried a long way by one tired person (where it was set down: four dents in soft ground and a medlar stone; a rung snapped off). They are 350 m apart: pick the marks' places on the causeway and tracks between them (`src/world.js` `CAUSEWAY_V4`, `src/v4/paths/routes.js`; brief 01 is joining the tracks up).
3. **The gold from the court.** The Sunken Court's floor (`src/structures/court.js`) shows where gold tesserae have been picked out: a patch of pale empty sockets in a pattern that makes the theft obvious (the sign's disc, in gold, half gone: the sign is a disc in a V notch, see the bible). One tessera already lies on the mole heap nearest the causeway (`src/v7/glyphs/moles.js`). Lay a sparse trail (a glint every 20–40 m, each within 3 m of a track or the causeway, bright enough to catch the eye from 10 m: small emissive tied to `ctx.sky.flicker`) to a hoard somewhere small and guilty that you choose: a jackdaw's or stoat's nest in a wall, a Twk-man's cache in a hollow gnomon, a pedlar's dropped purse. The thief should be funnier than the crime.

For each thread: both ends within 15 m of a track or the causeway; one merged vertex-coloured mesh per site where possible (the `Acc` accumulator in `src/v5/dressing/kit.js` is the pattern; `Painter` in `src/fauna/uncanny/kit.js` for cloth and bodies); colliders only where a walker would trip; clearances for the cover so props are not buried in grass (`excluded()` in `src/vegetation/ground.js` ~132; a pure exported `xClear(x, z)` like `collationClear`). Brief 02 is cropping the grass near the roads, which helps you. Add each end to the settings menu's teleports and give the thread's far end a place card only if a card would not give the joke away.

**`tools/threads-test.mjs`** in the suite: each thread's ends exist, stand on their ground, are within 15 m of a track or the causeway, and from the stated stand point of each end the other end or the next mark is not occluded by terrain where the brief says it should be seen.

**Not yours.** Tracks (01; if an end needs a track that does not exist, name it in your report and I will have it added), buildings (03), the house (05), threads 3, 5, 6 (later briefs).

**Done when** threads-test passes and there are two renders per end (from the approach at 15–25 m, and close), each of which a stranger could caption correctly without help.
