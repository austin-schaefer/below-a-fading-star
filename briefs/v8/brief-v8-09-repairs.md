# Brief v8-09: the roads' repairs and the sign made readable

**Goal.** Close what batch 1 left weaker than its briefs, so the critic finds a network with no dead country and a sign that can be read from where people stand. Read `plans/wilderness-v8.md`'s Status (the 15:02 entry lists these) and `briefs/v8/brief-v8-01-roads.md` and `brief-v8-03-strata.md` for what was asked.

**Build.**
1. **The canyon rim joined.** P13 and its four places (the Cutting, the Speculum, the Wrong Colour, the Tumbling Water) are off the network and exempt in `tools/roads-test.mjs` (`EXEMPT`), because the Span's fallen-bay colliders, the pit's rim and the deck's parapets wall that rim off. Find a real way in on foot from the causeway's end at the pit or from P14/P39/P40: walk it in the page (the test's own walkability probe, or `tools/walk-test.mjs`'s method) rather than trusting the map; open one gap in the rubble or cut one stair if there is none (a Late-folk ladder or a plank over a gap is in the bible's spirit; so is a Heliarch stair). Then remove the four places and P13 from the exemptions.
2. **Older leads rerouted.** P3, P4, P7, P8, P14, P15 and the causeway cross piers, walls and gateposts; roads-test notes them and does not fail. Print the list (which lead, which collider, where), move each lead's points round the obstacle or through an existing gap or gate (extending `pts` is safe; the wander seed is the array index, so never reorder `LEADS`), and for a wall with no gap make one (a stile, a broken panel): a road that meets a wall and stops is a lie. Where the causeway itself is blocked, move the obstacle, not the causeway. Then make the test fail on any blocked lead, old or new. `walkers-test` uses P3 and P2 by id and the Thurifer walks P3: rerun it.
3. **The gnomon's look.** The orchestrator's eye on `shots/wip/v8-01-a3-spawn.png` (in the deploy worktree's `shots/wip/`): the waystone nearest the spawn reads as a blue post with a bright ball on top, closer to a street lamp than to a leaning notched stone. The brief asked for a slim stone pillar 2.4–3 m tall, leaning a few degrees toward the sun, its head notched with the disc sitting in the notch, the disc a bead of ember glass that catches the sun. Make it stone-coloured in the Heliarchy's material (the v3 stone, `makeStoneMaterial` in `src/structures/materials.js`, hexagonal in section like the causeway's waymarkers), lean it, cut the notch so the V reads in silhouette against the sky at 30 m, seat the bead low in the notch and bring its glow down so it is a glint by day and only a small light in a gutter and in the wood (where they were too faint: let the bead be brighter under canopy). Compare before and after from the spawn, a junction and inside the wood.
4. **Heliarch roundels readable.** Brief 03's carved roundels (`src/v8/strata/`) read as a glowing dot beyond about 10 m. The sign must read as a disc in a notch from the track or the causeway nearest each placement: enlarge the notched head, darken the notch's cut, and let the disc be dull bronze by day with the glow reserved for the gutter. Check each of the Heliarch placements from its nearest road sample (`strata-test` knows them).
5. **The house's low tier.** `src/structures/taverna.js` costs about 76k triangles at `?q=low`, up from 52k before v8, with only one tile band dropped. Gate the frontage's small props, the planter and the voussoir count on quality so low is at or under 60k without changing the silhouette; brief 05 is working in `src/v8/house*` and makes one-line edits in `taverna.js`, so keep yours local to the low-tier branches and merge `origin/next` first.
