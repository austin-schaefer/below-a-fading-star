# Brief v8-05: the Pomegranate House, lit and lived in

**Goal.** One place on the map is warm, bright and silly, and makes everything else darker by being so. The owner: "almost all areas of the world have similar dark palette [...] similarly somber tone (no cuteness or humor to give the dark things punch)". The house's card already says it: "While we are alive we should sit among colored lights and taste good wines." Brief 03 gave it limewash, tile and an awning and left 28 empty lamp brackets (`src/structures/taverna.js`, `ctx.strata`, `src/v8/strata*`). You light it, people it and give it a voice. A visitor on the causeway at dusk should see it from 200 m as a cluster of coloured lights, walk toward it without being told to, and smile at least once when they get there.

**Build**, in `src/v8/house.js` and `src/v8/house/` (world module `house`; `?house=0`):
1. **Coloured lamps.** Glass lanterns in four colours (pomegranate red, saffron, turquoise, a green) on 03's brackets and strung on lines across the court and out along the approach. Emissive glass that blooms; they sway a little; they dim and flare together when the sun gutters (`ctx.sky.flicker`), a few go out and are relit late. Real light is limited to three live point lights map-wide (`src/lights.js` copies the nearest sources into three slots; `walkers-test` asserts at most three sources within reach + 20 m along every path, and the Leerie's standards and the Link-Boy are near): use one or two warm `PointLight`s for the court and fake the rest with emissive, a ground glow decal and warm vertex colour. From the causeway at 200 m the lamps must read as separate coloured points, not a smear: check the render.
2. **The house's people**, Late folk (the bible: patched colour, the sign sewn wrong), three to five figures with one small behaviour each, chosen to be funny in Vance's way (pompous, venal, exact), for example: a host who bows you in and bows you out with visibly different depths; two patrons at a table whose game one is losing, who both stop and stare until you look away; a musician who plays (see 3) and stops, offended, if you stand too close; someone asleep under a table with one bare foot out. `Painter` in `src/fauna/uncanny/kit.js` is the shared base; `src/v7/workers/rig.js` and `body.js` (`clothBody`, `head`, `sleeve`) are the cheapest rig for a seated or standing figure; `src/v7/workers/coheirs.js` is a model of a small two-figure behaviour. They have colliders and shadows, and stand or sit where a walker passing through the gate sees them.
3. **Thread 3 ends here** (`briefs/v8/index.md`): the brocade slipper on the causeway (`src/v5/dressing/slipper.js`, at -20.6, 25.74; canvas brocade `#25a085` with gold) has its mate here, and its owner: the sleeper under the table wears the mate on the other foot, or it hangs on a nail by the door as a pledge for an unpaid score. Your call; it must match at a glance.
4. **A voice.** Load the `sound-design` skill first and follow its method and gates. From 60 m: a murmur and now and then a laugh. Inside: the musician's instrument, a few plucked notes in D (the carillon's just minor pentatonic on D3, `src/audio/cues-v2.js` ~38), halting, with long rests, that stops when you crowd him; a cup set down; the lamps' glass ticking. It all drops out in a gutter and comes back raggedly, one nervous laugh first. Emitters at the figures, through the existing voice system (`src/audio/living.js` is the pattern). This is diegetic sound, not music: the music milestone (v9) comes later and is not yours. Add a pose at the house to `tools/audio-survey.json` and keep the gated survey green; run only your pose while iterating.
5. **The approach.** Trampled bare ground and a few spilt things outside the gate; benches; a string of lamps out to the kerb K1 and toward the causeway so the way in is lit.
