# Brief v8-06: the living belong to somebody

**Goal.** A visitor who has met two of the figures recognises the third as one of the same people, and can tell the two peoples apart at 30 m. The owner: "characters who seem connected, or at least that come from the same culture". Today the audit finds nine humanoids in nine unrelated costumes, most near-black (`#15110f`, `#16120f`, `#14110f`, `#17141a`), no emblem anywhere, and nothing any of them does that concerns another. Read the bible in `briefs/v8/index.md` ("The living", the sign, the strata).

**Build.**
1. **The Heliarchy's officers** (Thurifer `src/v7/walkers/thurifer.js`, Leerie `leerie.js`, Link-Boy `linkboy.js`, Taker of Altitudes `src/v7/workers/taker.js`, Sexton `sexton.js`, Stylite `src/v7/still/stylite.js`; and, as officers long past duty, the Long Sleeper `sleeper.js` and the Accrescent `accrescent.js`): one livery, worn differently by each. An **ember stole** (`#c8501e`, faded and frayed, darker where it is dirty), long, over the shoulders or as a sash or a belt or a rag round a hat, sized so it reads as a stroke of colour at 30 m; the **formal sign** (a disc in a V notch, Heliarch hand: `ctx.strata.sign('heliarch', ...)` in `src/v8/strata.js` if its geometry suits a badge, else your own to the bible's proportions) as a brass badge, a staff head, a buckle; and one shared cut (a tall collar, or a particular cap) that all of them have. Rank and office show: the Taker's robe is already orange (`#b07a42`), so he is the senior; the Link-Boy's stole is too big for him; the Sexton's is tucked in his belt out of the dirt; the Sleeper's has moss on it; the Accrescent's is half crystal. Lift the near-black coats a little toward a readable dark blue-grey so the figures separate from the ground now that brief 02 is changing it; v7.1 measured figure-to-ground contrast (`plans/wilderness-v5.md`, "### v7.1 visibility"): do not lose what it gained.
2. **The Late folk** (the Coheirs `src/v7/workers/coheirs.js`, the Wimpled Guest `src/v2/inhabitants/guest.js`; the house's people are brief 05's and follow the same rule): patched colour (two or three cloths per garment, pomegranate red, saffron, turquoise, undyed), and the sign sewn or painted **wrong** (the notch a crescent, the disc a pomegranate), small.
3. **Dealings.** At least three things that show the two peoples and the officers know each other, each visible from a track without being told: for example, the Leerie's oil flask is the house's (the same glass as its lamps) and he owes for it, a tally scratched on his first standard; the Thurifer bows to a gnomon as he passes one (brief 01 puts gnomons along the tracks: read them from `ctx` at run time, and bow only if one is within reach); the Sexton and the Taker have the same staff head; the Coheirs are mending a net with a strip of ember cloth in it; a spare stole hangs on a peg at the Kept Fire for the keeper who is not there. Small, exact, and at least one of them funny.
4. **The deodand and the Bather** stay as they are: not people. The Twk-men are brief 07's.

**How.** No single figure kit exists; three rigs share `Painter` (`src/fauna/uncanny/kit.js`): `src/v7/walkers/gait.js` (parts posed into `uParts`; a stole is one more part or a loft on the torso part), `src/v7/workers/rig.js` and `body.js` (`clothBody`'s hem band reaches three workers at once), `src/v7/still/kit.js`. If a small shared helper (`src/v8/livery.js`: the stole's loft, the badge, the colours) keeps the nine edits consistent, write it. Keep each figure one mesh as now; no new draw calls per figure. Cloth that hangs should move a little with the existing hem sway where the rig has one.

**Tests.** `walkers-test`, `workers-test`, `still-test`, `small-test`, `regions-test` keep passing at their counts (some read part counts: update a count you change on purpose and say so). Add to `tools/strata-test.mjs` or a new `tools/livery-test.mjs` in the suite: every officer's mesh has the stole's colour in its vertex colours and a badge part; every Late figure has at least three cloth colours.

**Done when** the tests pass and there is a sheet of all the officers at the same framing at 10 m and at 30 m (before and after, same poses: `tools/views-v7-walkers.json`, `views-v7-workers.json`, `views-v7-still.json` have the poses), a sheet of the Late folk, and one render per dealing; on the 30 m sheet the stole is visible on every officer.
