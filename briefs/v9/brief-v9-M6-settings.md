# Brief v9-M6: the music joins the walk, off by default; the volume sliders

The owner has listened to all nine tracks and does not want them heard by first-time visitors: "ship the music, but have it off by default and labeled in settings as experimental". A second music milestone will try again. So this brief makes what exists safe to ship and easy for the owner to try, and adds the volume sliders they asked for. `next` has M1 (patches, player) and M2 (director, glue, bus, setting, `?music=`) merged together for the first time: nobody has run them together yet.

Read first: `AGENTS.md`, the `wilderness`, `sound-design` and `music` skills, `plans/wilderness-v9.md` (Status, top entries), `src/audio/music/` (all of it), `src/audio/graph.js`, the settings menu's code and `tools/settings-test.mjs`.

**1. Make it work together.** The glue (`src/audio/music/index.js`) was written against a stub; make it call the real `player.js`. The reviewer's notes for the glue: one `play()` at a time, stop the previous; `play()` throws synchronously on an unknown voice id; a stem at gain 1 is about −35 LUFS, set the mix on the bus; `stop()` resolves about fade + 0.4 s later; `fadeTo` does nothing after stop; pace from the handle's `t` and `done`, not wall-clock timers (the clock freezes while the context is suspended). Add all nine tracks to `catalog.js` with their pools and specials (moor, wood, wet, cold, garden, dry; span, keep, gates). `?music=<track>` plays any of them 3 s in, whatever the setting says; the debug panel lists them.

**2. Off by default, experimental.** The Music setting's default is **off**. Its row reads "Music (experimental)", with one short plain line under it for a visitor ("Early music written by the agents that built this place. Off unless you turn it on."; improve the words). With it off, nothing of the music is built, fetched beyond the module itself, or running: no oscillators, no timers, no per-frame work beyond one cheap check. Keep the frequency choice (rare / default / frequent) for when it is on: the owner likes it.

**3. The volume sliders.** One Sound group in the settings menu with a clear visual hierarchy: the master volume and mute first and largest; under it, set visibly as its parts (indented or lighter, smaller labels), three sliders: **Music** (`musicBus`), **Sound effects** (footsteps, one-shot cues, figures: `dryBus`, `cueBus`), **Ambience** (wind, the drone, place and region beds: `windBus`, `droneBus` and whatever else is a bed: read the graph and say what you put where). The music frequency row sits with the Music slider. Each defaults to 100%, is persisted like the other settings, ramps (no zipper noise, no click), and at 100% leaves the mix exactly as it is today (`audio-test` unchanged). The Music slider and frequency are dimmed while music is off. It must work on a phone: render the menu at 1280×720 and at 390×844 and look at both.

**4. The flute.** The owner on four tracks: "the flute is PIERCING. really loud, kind of abrasive. i wouldn't want the flute to die entirely, it sets a nice mood". In `patches.js`: bring it down and take the edge off (lower lowpass, softer attack, less breath noise up high, a little less level against the pad); render `cold` and `dry` before and after with `tools/music-render.mjs` and compare the 2–5 kHz band and the per-voice level (`--solo`). Add a per-voice harshness reading to the render tool if there is none, so one abrasive instrument cannot hide in a quiet mix. Write new listening copies (peak −6 dBFS) for all nine as `shots/wip/music/v3-<track>-listen.wav`.
