1. **The stilters** (the code's name; a visitor meets no name), in the grove in the new north wood (`GROVE` in `src/v18/plan.js`, (-198, 22), r 22; F1 is growing the wood there now, so until it merges you stand on moor: build against the coordinates). Four or five tall things, 9 to 13 m, that stand among the trunks as trunks: two or three legs like boles with bark and moss on them, a body high up in the crown where a knot might be a face, long arms held up like boughs with real leaves on them. Standing still they pass for trees to a visitor walking by; what gives them away is that **they move only when nobody is looking**: when a stilter is outside the view (and not within a few metres), it takes a step or two: nearer, or across the path behind you, or simply turned to face where you are. Look back and a trunk is where no trunk was; look up and the knot is looking down. They never touch the visitor, never block a path shut, never move in view, not even at the edge of the frame (test the frustum with a margin, and the phone's tall view). One of them is asleep and really is nearly a tree: something has nested in it. When the sun gutters, in the dark, they all move at once, and when the light comes back they are standing in a ring round the visitor, facing in, still. This is the beat a visitor tells a friend about: make it work and make it fair (a ring with a gap toward the track).
   The dread is in the stillness and the scale. Not ents, not monsters with glowing eyes, not horror-game jump scares: old, patient, curious. Their step, when it is heard and not seen, is F5's (sound) to make: expose an event (`ctx.events` or what `world-code` names) when one steps.

2. **Eyes.** Along the three spurs (`SPURS`) and in the old wood off the road: pairs of small pale eyes low between the trunks at 15 to 40 m that shut, or sink, when the visitor's view comes onto them, and open again elsewhere. Many kinds of spacing and height (something small, something long and low, once something with three). Never a body. Cheap: instanced, no lights. Sparse enough to be doubted.
