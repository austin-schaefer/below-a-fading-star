# v18, for every agent

Read `AGENTS.md`, then load `wilderness` and `world-code` (with `runtime.md` and `modules.md`), `visual-art` (with the reference your thing needs: `land.md`, `architecture.md`, `figures.md`, light and colour) for anything a visitor sees, and `storytelling` if your brief points at a story file. Read `plans/wilderness-v18.md` (short; its survey has the numbers), `stories/the-graffer.md` (the story everything here belongs to), the owner's issue (`gh issue view 18 --json body,title`, the body only) and `briefs/v18/index.md` (the plan's coordinates and levels, who owns what).

Rules for this milestone:

- **The machine is the owner's and it is in use.** One headless browser at a time, renders and tests in sequence; `nice -n 10` in front of every `node tools/...`; no `--trace`. Serve your own worktree on your own port and set `FP_BASE=http://localhost:<your port>` for every tool that loads the page (`tools/views.mjs` and others default to 8123, the working branch's server: the planner rendered against it by mistake). Never serve, check out or edit anything in `.claude/worktrees/deploy`. Kill your server and any browser when you hand back.
- **Waiting on a run:** never a `pgrep -f` loop (`wilderness`, "Waiting on a run"). Wait on a process id or a line in a log, and never leave a waiter behind.
- **One plan file.** Every coordinate of this milestone is in `src/v18/plan.js` (three-free; imports nothing), written by the orchestrator. Read your numbers from it; add rows only in your own section; never change another brief's rows without saying so in your report. New code goes in new files under `src/v18/`. The story is `stories/the-graffer.md`: keep it true to what you build (its State, Beats and Log).
- **Others are at work beside you** (the index says who owns what: F1 the ground, the trees and the tracks; F2 the glasshouse; F3 under the ground; F4 creatures). Keep edits to `src/main.js`, `src/world.js`, `src/settings.js`, `src/controls.js`, `src/post.js`, `src/height/field.js`, `src/height/floor.js`, `src/water.js`, `src/water/river.js`, the audio graph and the skill references to the lines your brief names.
- **The wood is at the low tier's limit.** The survey (`plans/wilderness-v18.md`) measured about 33 ms of GPU a frame in the wood on low, the tier's whole budget, at 186 to 209 draw calls and 1.5 to 1.8 M triangles. Whatever you add is paid for: stay inside the module budgets (`world-code`, about 25 calls and 400k triangles a module, far less on low), cull hard by distance, cast no new shadows on low, and measure before and after at your stand with the perf tooling `runtime.md` names. Nothing new in `heightAt`, `biomeAt` or `waterLevelAt` outside one box test.
- **Moving the ground moves what stands on it.** Any change to `heightAt`, `biomeAt`, a region's weights or what scatter reads runs `threads`, `seams`, `geometry`, `roads`, `paths`, `outline`, `regions`, `rocks`, `dressing`, `strata` (`ONLY=... tools/suite.sh <port>`) and `walkers` (`nice -n 10 node tools/walkers-test.mjs $B`, not in the suite: 19/20 on v16, P12's pit-lamps red on `main` too). v16's floor left a shrine unbuilt, a path's end under water and a tree in a doorway, found only by the batch's suite. `seams` and `geometry`: list the rows you would re-record and why; do not re-record wholesale.
- **Judged moving, and judged in the dark.** Anything that moves or that a visitor does is looked at in frames taken in order with your report shut (`tools/step-frames.mjs`, or `views.mjs` with a `pre`), at medium and low. The wood is near black: a thing nobody can see is not built. Every find must read in its own frame at the distance a visitor meets it, by its own light, without turning the wood into day. A blind reader (a helper on `sonnet`, shown only the frames, asked "what is this, and does anything look wrong?") sees each round before you hand back; for figures and creatures also render the study page's sheet (`study.html`, `tools/study-shots.mjs`; register what you make).
- **A fault is fixed at its cause, and its kind is guarded:** add a row to an existing test where one fits (`outline` for water, `edges` and `under-geometry` for under the ground, `heights` for a way up or down).
- **Names.** At most three new names a visitor meets in this milestone: the Florilegium (F2), the Lower Beds (F3), and none else without the orchestrator's word. Each goes in `LANDMARKS` with its `line` empty (the owner gives quotations; an agent never writes one) and on the map only if your brief says so. The tone: Vance's Dying Earth, baroque and worn; nothing new, nothing cartoon, nothing generic fantasy. Not the first idea: something a visitor would walk 200 m to look at and could find in no other game.
- **Phones.** Render the low tier; stay inside the module budgets (`world-code`). Nothing in this milestone may make the low tier slower; under the ground, the seal (`below.keep`, `LAYER_BELOW`) is what keeps a room cheap.
- **Tests before handing back:** the ones your change touches and any you add, by name: `ONLY=name,name tools/suite.sh <port>` (say the counts; zero tests is a failure). Never the whole suite on a branch.
- **Renders** go in `shots/wip/` as `v18-<brief>-<stage>-<view>.png`. The survey's frames of the wood as it was are `shots/wip/v18-survey-*.png` in the deploy worktree (read them there; never write there). Render your own stands before you change anything (`-before-`) and after every round. Look at every frame before you name it; never delete one.
- **Helpers run small.** Frames handed to helper agents go to `haiku` or `sonnet`, never your own model.
- Document in `world-code` (`runtime.md` or `modules.md`), present tense, a few lines, what the next agent needs; keep the story file you build from true to what is built (its State, Beats and Log).
- Commit and push after every part (never `--no-verify`, never force). Write your report into `briefs/v18/reports/<branch>.md` and push it. Hand back as `world-code` says, in under 250 words: what changed, the commands and counts, the frames to look at in order (paths), what is still weak, any default you took, and a `?pos=` link the owner can open on the working branch's server (8171) to see it.

- **Ports.** Serve only on the port your brief names; first check `lsof -ti:<port>` is empty (the owner's other projects use 8123 and 8160). The working branch is served on 8171, not 8123: every link you give the owner is `http://localhost:8171/...`.
