# Brief v10-A1: the crystal and the door

Read `briefs/v10/_common.md` first; it is half of this brief. Port **8163**, worktree `.claude/worktrees/issue-1002-central`.

Two of the map's central things, each the end of a walk that today ends in nothing (issue #9 names both: "the big crystal in center of the lattice; the door at the bottom of the staircase").

**1. The mother crystal at the heart of the Lattice** (`src/vegetation/mother.js`: 60 m tall, glacial blue; the Lattice hums on A4, and the critic measured a steady tone there at 500 Hz). A visitor who walks in through the crystal field to its foot can lay a hand on it. What answers is yours to invent; the fixed points: it is the crystal that answers (light moving in it, up its 60 m, out into the field's other crystals; sound in the Lattice's own pitch, not a new instrument), it can be watched from the foot by looking up and is also seen from far across the map by anyone standing there, and it is different the second visit from the first (the count is yours to use). The sun's guttering is the world's one shared event (`ctx.sky.flicker`); an answer that has something to do with it would tie the place to the rest.

**2. The Hypogeum's door** (`src/v2/ruins/hypogeum.js`: a tomb cut 4 m into the ground, a stair down, a bronze door under a lintel of glyphs that shift one place at each gutter; `src/v7/glyphs/` has fish in the tarn that form the door's last glyph, and the lens mount elsewhere has a door ajar and lit). At the bottom of the stair the visitor can knock. The door does not open: what is behind it stays unseen. Something answers, from inside. Decide what, and what the third knock on the third visit gets that the first did not. `stories/` has no story for the Hypogeum; this act is its first beat, so write the file (who is in there is yours; `stories/README.md`'s "People who could be the same person" first).

Two acts, two commits or more, one hand-back. If one is going badly, finish the other well and say so.
