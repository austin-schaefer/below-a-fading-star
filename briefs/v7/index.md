## Design gaps (questions, each with the default the briefs use)

The briefs build the default; the orchestrator logs each in `MORNING.md` so Austin can reverse it. The first three change the most.

1. **The Link-Boy across P1–P17: one figure, or one per lead, and how does he "meet you"?** The idea: he "meets you on the lead paths ... and walks ahead"; the plan: "waits at the Lynchets' east wall (−165, −105); walks P1–P17". *Default:* one figure for the map. He waits at his post on P4; when you are on, or heading for the end of, any path lead (not rivulets, not K1), he is placed just off that end, or 10 m ahead of you on it, only while the spot is out of view and at least 40 m off; within 12 m he walks ahead to the lead's far end, stands aside, walks back, and returns unseen to his post. One lead at a time, each at most once in 5 minutes.
2. **The Link-Boy's moving shadows: real or drawn?** The idea keeps the point light "for its moving shadows"; here a point light casts none, and a shadow-casting light would recompile every lit shader or add a shadow pass everywhere (the Kept Fire agent's finding). *Default:* drawn: a ground disc round him while he walks, with the light's pool swinging with his stride and soft wedges behind the Lynchets' wall runs and boulders within 10 m, plus a sliding noise for the heather. A real shadow-casting light is a later, measured call.
3. **Audio ownership: do the figure agents write their own sounds?** The run says "then the audio pass over everything new". *Default:* yes to the pass. The figure agents fire named cues at the moments the ideas give and write no synthesis, so the figures are silent until v7-07 lands. The one exception is the Repetend's echo (v7-05), because the echo is the behaviour.
4. **The Link-Boy's pace.** The walker walks at 3.6 m/s and hurries at 6.6. *Default:* he holds 10 m up to walking pace with a long stride. If you hurry he lets the gap close to 5 m and holds that. He never runs.
5. **The Link-Boy's sound.** The idea gives none ("He never speaks"). *Default:* quiet footsteps by ground and a faint flutter of the link within 8 m (physical, not content). The alternative is silence.
6. **The Leerie before a gutter.** A row takes about 140 s and gutters come every 60–110 s, so a gutter usually catches him mid-row. *Default:* if he reaches the end first, he stands at the last standard until a gutter has come and ended, then turns. The row's lighting stays the lights module's (its wave); he never lights a lamp.
7. **The Thurifer's turn at the shrine.** The man in the cloak of eyes stands about 6 m out from the shrine's niche, at P3's start. *Default:* it turns at the first P3 sample at least 8 m from him.
8. **The Thurifer's bow trigger.** *Default:* within 10 m and closing, or within 5 m. It steps 1.6 m off the track and bows 75°. It rises once you are 3 m past along the track or 14 m away.
9. **The Taker's "between him and the sun".** At 13.5° elevation the true line of sight is 6 m up by the road. *Default:* bearing within ±3° of the sun's azimuth, 1.5–60 m away (so road walkers trip it, as the plan intends). He sits on the pavement; the idea names no seat ("a book on his knee").
10. **The Coheirs' reaction.** *Default:* once per approach (re-armed beyond 30 m), not held while you stay within 20 m. The hum is a third (v7-07 picks major or minor once, for good).
11. **The Sexton's graves without a heightfield cut.** The idea offers "shallow decals, or a marked block". Since the Nave, the rule is that the heightfield is never carved. *Default:* the Nave's method: the terrain triangles over the block are taken out, and his own ground mesh goes in with real holes 1.8 m deep, colliders on each, and cover and rocks cleared after the draws. The plan is 60 slots, 40 open at load, one more per gutter, kept for the session, ending at 60. Spoil heaps beside each are allowed as the physical consequence.
12. **The Sexton's site.** The table says (55, 272), `routes.js` `AVOID` holds (55, 274) r 18, and the graves span z 264–284 (centre 274). *Default:* the figure goes at (55, 272) and the block at z 264–284; `AVOID` is left alone.
13. **The Long Sleeper's medlar.** Is there "a single old medlar" at (180, −40)? *Default:* if `trees.js` planted one within 4 m, he lies under it. If not, his module builds one from the Bletting's medlar form, and the Bletting's planting list stays untouched (adding to it would move every later tree).
14. **The Stylite's hauling.** He is "unmoving", yet the basket "rises ... to his hand". *Default:* his hand does not move. The rope rises through his loose hand and the slack coils beside him: oblique, not mechanical. The trigger is 18 m from the basket, re-armed beyond 40 m.
15. **The Accrescent's hour and trail.** *Default:* one step per 3600 s of session time (so at most one in a usual visit). There are about 115 prints from where the Lattice's crystal ends to his rear foot, each with a small tuft of prisms so the line reads from 50–60 m (Austin's "distinct trail").
16. **The Scriveners' "the glyph the bronze door showed last".** *Default:* the form most recently pushed off the lintel's end (`glyphOrder[0]` after a shift); before the first shift, the last slot's form.
17. **The Scriveners on near-black water.** "Small dark fish" vanish under the tarn's #0e0a12. *Default:* the formed shoal lies at the surface and breaks the water's sheen (rings, or dark notches in the reflected sky). Two attempts, reported honestly.
18. **The glyph motif's extent.** *Default:* exactly the plan's three: one glyph engraved on each standing brass cap (four; the tarn's deep one is absent), no two neighbours alike; one larger gold tessera, face up on the heap nearest the causeway; one glyph about 35 cm tall scratched on the erratic's sun face at (75, −200). Nine forms only, no new ones.
19. **The moles: seen or not?** Austin: "more of the cute". The idea shows only the hills. *Default:* no mole is ever shown; the cute is in the scale, the slow heave and the click. Rule 2 ("Nothing cute") gives way to Austin's note here, as it did for the stoats.
20. **The moles' heap cycle.** *Default:* a pool of 30 slots, 18 at load, a new heap every 50–110 s while you are within 35 m; the oldest flattens out of view once all are used.
21. **The Indistinct at the arc's ends and when you are far.** *Default:* within 30 m of the ditch it holds exactly 7.0 m (straight-line), on its own side of you. When you are more than 7 m from the ditch it waits at the ditch point nearest you. At an end it thins and re-forms 7 m beyond you, out of view if it can. Speed cap 8 m/s.
22. **The Simulacrum: "a long grave face the size of a man's".** *Default:* life-size (a man's face, about 0.22 × 0.32 m), read from 4–7 m in the gutter. The grammar allows "the size of a man", which would be far easier to read; Austin may prefer that. If the face fails after two attempts, the columns stay and the face goes behind `?simulacrum=face`, logged as dropped per his note.
23. **The Bather's swap.** "A swap between two models by the player's height". *Default:* she shows while your feet are 7 m or more above the pool's water and you are 10 m or more from her (hysteresis 1.5 m). The swap happens only out of view or behind the canyon wall; otherwise, after 4 s, a 1.2 s dithered cross-fade, never a pop. The stones lie where her masses lie.
24. **The Luminous Reach's sources and strength.** *Default:* sources wherever the ribbon is faster than about 1.6 m/s or rough, plus the Speculum's churn; decay time about 70 s of travel; a plume 50–70 m into the fen only (the tarn untouched). The gutter gain is 1 + 2.5 × (1 − flicker), checked by render to be the brightest line on the floor. The colour is its own blue-green (not the wisps, the moss or the Lattice).
25. **The Toadstone's ledge.** *Default:* a near-flat ledge on the drawn west wall within 2 m of (180, 230), darkened and glossed as wet. The stone's glow is emissive only, about 0.8 × (1 − flicker)^1.5, no light.
26. **Module names and flags.** *Default:* `living-<group>` (so `shadows.js`'s rule catches them), `src/v7/<group>.js`, `?<group>=0` plus a flag per figure, `ctx.<group>`, `__fp.<group>.*` test hooks. Each group writes its own `tools/<group>-test.mjs` rather than sharing one file.
27. **Figure collisions.** *Default:* still figures get static colliders. Walkers get none while walking (they step aside or lead), except a moving r 0.3 one while the Thurifer bows (`controls.js` reads the collider list every frame, so writing `x, z` moves it).
