## Goal

Where the village meets the Pomegranate House and the open valley: the level ground below the lowest riser, round the house's east and south sides to its gate. Today a visitor coming down through the village arrives at the house's blank south wall. Yours: the plots the plan gives `pennyworth` (stalls, the garden, the houses along the lane, the carrier-house plot), the lowest riser itself, the lanes to the gate, and the house's south side.

Set pieces, bend them if you find better:
1. **The house's south door.** A door let into the Pomegranate House's south wall where the Heliarch stair's foot and the ramp arrive, plainly later than the wall (a ragged cut dressed with a horseshoe arch in newer plaster, a step worn already), opening into the court. `house-test`, the house's lamps and its folk must stay true; keep the gate in the north wall as it is. Read `stories/` for who is in the court and do not walk the door through anyone's place.
2. **The lowest riser, rebuilt.** V0 left the Demesne's sunk ha-ha wall standing in the sector with a ramp through it. Make it the Elder stair's lowest riser, or what later hands made of it: Elder block at the foot, a Heliarch ashlar repair, a Late buttress, lean-to stalls built against it under plank roofs.
3. **The Pennyworth market**: not barrels and crates. Stalls that are a plank on two Heliarch drums, a cloth on poles, a counter that is a keep block; what is sold here is small and odd and comes in batch 3 (salt by the thumb, news, lamp oil, slippers): build the stalls and their bare boards. The salt-weigher's stall has a perch and a peg at thumb height (the Twk-men: `stories/README.md`, `twk-test` must stay green; their salt pile at the house's gate stays).
4. **The wreck.** On the carrier-house plot, the one vessel form of this quarter: the `wreck`, a vast vessel fallen and broken at the end of the main's line, half sunk, someone living in the sound half, a garden in the broken half.
5. **The garden**: beans on poles, a well-head that is a Heliarch drum, and the Tesserine Moles, whom V0 moved here from the lawn the village now stands on (`glyphs-test`): their heaps in the beds, each with its tessera, are the gardener's quarrel.
6. **The lanes round the house** to its north gate, east and west, lit by the house's own lamps and one or two of yours; where the east lane meets the Leerie's standards (kerb K1), the village ends and the road begins: mark the edge (two posts, a last lamp).
