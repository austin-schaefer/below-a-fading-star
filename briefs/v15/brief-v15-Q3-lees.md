# Brief v15-Q3: the Lees (the bench: the square, the fountain of noon, the shops)

## Goal

The village's main square, on the bench at the foot of the Elder steps, with the Strake along its upper side and the Heliarch stair's foot at its east end: the place a visitor arrives in and understands they are somewhere people live. The owner asked for "a square or two, a fountain perhaps, some possible shops". Yours: the square and its paving, the fountain, the shop plots on the bench and in the Low Rung's vaults (`lees-*`), the old way's arrival from the north, and the join to the Strake (which stands; leave its buildings, finish the ground in front of them into the square).

Set pieces, bend them if you find better:
1. **The fountain of noon.** Not water. The Noonwrights' line came down this hill carrying stored daylight, and where it broke the light still wells: a great vessel's broken foot set in the square as a basin (the `sunk` form at its largest, or your own), in whose bottom a little daylight pools and brims, white-gold and steady (it breathes, it never flickers: `visual-art/architecture.md`), lighting the faces of the square from below. People come to it with flasks as to a well: a worn kerb, a dipping chain, flasks left to fill, the Leerie's slate of what is owed (he draws his lamp oil here: `stories/README.md`). It is the one bright thing in a square that never sees the sun, and it should be seen from the road. It has to read as light held in a bowl, not a glowing disc: look at how the Noon Road's lit carriers pool light low (`src/v14/noon-road.js`).
2. **Shops in the vaults**: the Low Rung's riser opened as round Heliarch arches (the kit's undercroft, in-riser form; the vaults are V0's), each closed a different way (a shutter, a counter, a plank wall with a wicket, a curtain), a sign each in the Late hand (`lateSign`), a bare shelf or peg for what batch 3 will sell: a dead-letter counter with pigeonholes, a slipper-maker's window, a vintner's twelve hooks, a salt-weigher's scale with a perch too small for any bird, a picture-framer's empty frames. Build the furniture; leave the goods.
3. **The square's floor**: Heliarch hexagons where the processional way came through, robbed out in patches and made good in cobble and earth; a drain; a tree or a vine on poles if anything grows in this shade; benches where the wall is warm.
4. **The arrival**: from the old way in from the north the visitor sees the fountain's light first, framed by something (two leaning posts, an arch of the old order with one voussoir gone).

The fountain is the only vessel form here.

## Done when

- From the road at 100 m at the gutter the square is found by the fountain's light; standing in it, the light is steady, pooled, and does not blow out the frame on high or vanish on low (histograms, both).
- Every shopfront reads as a shop from across the square without its goods.
