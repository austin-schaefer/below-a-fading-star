# Wilderness modules

What each module under `src/` owns. Every agent that adds a module extends this table by one row; a module that changes what it owns updates its row.

`src/world.js` is the map: `LANDMARKS` (each with a centre, radius, name and a line of flavour: the owner's quotations, set exactly as given (v14-B5, #52); one may have an `axis`, a line along x (or, with `z0`, `z1`, along z) it is the distance to, and `at`, where the debug panel lands you; a `card` is a name card that wins inside a broad landmark; `feet: [lo, hi]` shows a card only while the walker's feet are at that height, a deck's name not shown on the floor under it; `broad` makes a card give way to every other card inside it (Titan's Causeway, the whole Span deck and both stairs' tops, under the Long Wait and the Shaky Splice); `reached` names the way that reaches a place no track does, for roads-test), the `CAUSEWAY` polyline, `WATER` bodies, `SPAWN`, `SUN` (fixed direction) and `PALETTE`; and for v4, `PEAKS` (the named peaks on the walls), `REGIONS` (the bioregions' shapes), `SITES` (every reserved v4-v6 site, not yet landmarks) and `CAUSEWAY_V4` (the road from the colossi's feet to the pit: `ctx.causeway` and `causewayDistance` use it; `CAUSEWAY` stays v3's stretch for the bench and the things placed by it). Names marked **[Proposed]** are inherited placeholders; names here are yours to settle (`AGENTS.md`, "The piece and the record").

## Who owns each shared cost

A cost no single world module owns has one owner and one place its number is read. `?perf=1` rows are the overlay's (and the bench's `rows`); load phases are `tools/load-probe.mjs`'s table (`src/startup.js` names them); `tools/bench-report.mjs` also prints, after its tables, each module over the budget in `world-code/SKILL.md` ("Light and budgets") at any station.
