# Curation brief: screenshots

Hand this to a mid-tier subagent before every merge to `main`. Fill in the two bracketed parts.

---

You are curating the screenshot archive at `shots/` (branch `[branch]`). The owner tells the story of the build from these renders (how the scene was built, stage by stage, with agents), so the keepers must tell the story and there must not be hundreds of them. Read `shots/README.md` (the naming scheme, the stage table and the captioned index of what is already kept) and the `wilderness` skill's Renders (`.agents/skills/wilderness/SKILL.md`: how renders are named and how they reach `shots/wip/`).

1. **Leave alone** any file still being produced by a running agent: prefixes `[list the prefixes of agents currently running]`, anything newer than when you start, `shots/README.md`, `shots/wip/README.md`, and `shots/plan-*.png`.
2. **Look at every other image in `shots/wip/`** (gitignored; work in the deploy worktree's folder, where `tools/worktree.sh rm` gathers the agents' renders) with the Read tool (it shows you the picture). Classify: keeper (a distinct subject or a story beat, the best of its near-duplicates), duplicate, or junk (black, blank, broken, debug maps). Keep roughly one in five. Always keep: the first render of a new module even if ugly; every before/after pair that shows a decision; each new landmark and creature at its best view; up to five of the funniest failures.
3. **Convert keepers into `shots/`** as WebP (`magick in.png -quality 85 out.webp`; `shots/` takes no PNG) under the scheme `NN-subject-view[-before|after].png`: `NN` is the stage number from the stage table (add a row if this hand-back is a new stage), then the subject word so that files about the same thing sort together, then the view. Lowercase, hyphens, extension `.webp`. Then **delete from `shots/wip/` everything you looked at**, keepers included (they now live in `shots/`), and every non-image file beside them (frame lists, `.bin` dumps, WAVs, scratch scripts): when you finish, the inbox holds only what step 1 told you to leave. A curation that leaves the inbox full has not happened; `tools/wip-check.sh` prints its size.
4. **Use `git rm` for deletes inside `shots/`** so history keeps the originals. Do not commit; the orchestrator commits. Touch nothing outside `shots/`.
5. **Update `shots/README.md`**: the stage table and a one-line caption for every kept image, in sorted order.
**Deleting from `shots/wip/` is allowed, standing** (the owner, 2026-11-20: "you have standign permission to delte wip renders"). It covers raw renders and tool output under `shots/wip/` in any worktree of this repository (gitignored files only), by the curator or the orchestrator, with no asking. It does not cover anything under `shots/` that is committed (step 4), or anything outside this repository. If a permission check still refuses the curator's delete, the curator hands back the exact list and the orchestrator deletes it itself, on this standing word, after looking at what the list names.

6. Hand back counts (kept, moved, deleted), the megabytes `tools/wip-check.sh` prints before and after, the stage table rows you added, and any image you could not interpret.
