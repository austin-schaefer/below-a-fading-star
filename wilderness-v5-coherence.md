# Wilderness v5: coherence

Every v5 thing rendered at the high tier, 1280×720, at the same time of day (the gutter held off unless noted), and judged against the register in `wilderness-v4.md`: old, worn, vast, oblique; Vance's Dying Earth and Hodgson; nothing shiny, nothing new, no game wall; instruments and civic works in dressed stone, bronze, black glass and lattice. The mountains are not judged here (`?mountains=` is Austin's pending pick), nor anything built before v5, except where it shares a frame with a v5 thing.

## 2. The clashes and drifts, ranked

1. **The Leerie's standards read as a street of Victorian lamp posts** (`38-coh-leerie-victorian-before.png`, `38-fix-leerie-mantle-after.png`). The cross-arm and the capped four-sided lantern are the one domestic, modern-period silhouette in the bowl, and they stand in a ruled line on the Demesne's turf. Proposed fix: a re-brief of the standard's head in `src/v5/lights.js` (`lantern()` and the cross-arm): drop the cross-arm, make the head an open iron cage or a bronze cup round the mantle, perhaps a staff that leans; small (one function), but a design call, so not done here.
2. **The Battlemented Ridge reads as built crenellation at distance** (`v5-12-coh-deck-000.png`, `v5-12-coh-occultation.png`, `v5-12-coh-ridge.png`). The square stubs in an even row on the north crest look like a castle wall's merlons from the deck and the air, against v4's "nothing with crenellations". Proposed fix: a re-brief of the block in `src/v4/mountains.js` (vary the stubs' widths and heights, break the rhythm, lean a few) after your mountains pick, since that file is yours now.
3. **The Cincture's rim outshines the failing sun in the gutter** (`v5-12-coh-gutter.png`). By its brief it "brightens as the sky darkens"; at a 0.3 flicker the rim keeps 0.55 + 0.45 × 0.3 of its sunlit red and gains a hot ember on top, so it is the brightest line in the sky while the land is at 30%. If you want the gutter to be the sun failing everywhere, the fix is one number in `src/v5/cincture.js` (the rim's `0.55 + 0.45 * uFlicker` to `0.2 + 0.8 * uFlicker`, and the gutter ember's `0.55, 0.2, 0.09` halved); left as built because the brief asks for it.
4. **The tower and the Galleries tangle from the Lynchets** (`v5-12-coh-tower.png`). From the terraces, where the tower's whole silhouette is meant to stand against the glow, the Galleries' ironwork on the wall behind fills the space round the ring, and the lens mount reads as one more mast of the gantry. From the causeway and spawn they separate. Proposed fix: none small; move the view people take (the terraces' path) a few metres south, or accept it.
5. **The v2 sky lattices crowd the Cincture** (`v5-12-coh-ridge.png`, `v5-12-coh-cincture.png`, `v5-12-coh-gutter.png`). The high-air lattices of `src/skylife/lattices.js` (v2) now hang right beside the Cincture's arc in the north-east, two built things in the same patch of sky at different scales, and one floats over the saddle in the Thrones' frame (`v5-12-coh-thrones.png`; checked with `?galleries=0`: it is the sky lattice, not the Galleries). Proposed fix: a number in `lattices.js` (move the lattices' bearings out of 30–110°, or their count down by one); pre-v5, so not touched here.

At 45% the ironwork kept almost its full contrast at 400 m and read pasted in front of the wall it hangs on, from spawn, the causeway and the spit. Pairs: `38-fix-galleries-before.png` / `-after.png` (the causeway), `v5-12-coh-fix-galleries-spit-before.png` / `-after.png` (the spit).

The near-white mantles were the whitest light in the bowl, off the dusk's palette beside the post lamp (`#ff8a3a`) and the doorway (`#ffae5c`); they now burn amber and the halos warm. Pair: `38-fix-leerie-mantle-before.png` / `-after.png` (gutter held at 0.3).
