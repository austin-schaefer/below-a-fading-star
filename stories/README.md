# Stories

The world's library: what has happened in this country, one file a story. A tool, not a gate: worth a look before inventing something a visitor will find, and yours to add to. How to write one, what makes a find a story and not a prop, and how they are made to touch: the `storytelling` skill (`.agents/skills/storytelling/SKILL.md`). The shape of a file: `_template.md`.

Anyone may add a story, add a beat to another's, or repair one, and says so in that story's Log. A story's file is kept true to what is built in the same commit that builds or changes it.

Six plain, one deep. The library is for visitors and for the agents who design the world: a story belongs here if it makes things hang together, read or not.

## People who could be the same person

Keep this list: it is how stories come to touch. Before inventing someone, look here.

- The eleven who dine at the Collation. Unseen.
- Whoever commissioned the painter. Unseen.
- The pedlar whose sack split. Unseen.
- The Sexton, the Leerie (the lamplighter), the Little Thurifer, the Link-Boy, the host of the Pomegranate House and its patrons, the Bather, the astronomer: all walk or stand in the world and none has a story yet.

## Things that travel

- Saffron cloth with a cream lining and a madder border (the traveller).
- Gold tesserae from the court's sun (the jackdaw); a pewter spoon and a bronze ring in its nest, owners unknown.
- A medlar: painted by the painter, a core on the chair's trail, an orchard of them at the Bletting.
- Salt.
- The keeper's charcoal and the tally: one stroke for every traveller who stopped at the Drum.
