# The door on her back

- **Kind:** plain
- **State:** built

## The story

She has had enough of the Lappet, or it of her. She is going east along the old kerb, and she has taken her front door with her, tied on her back with two cords and a bundle on top of it, so that nobody else can live in her house. A door is heavy; every few steps she stops and shrugs it higher, and she has stopped now, just past the last lamp, and is looking back over her shoulder at the hill.
