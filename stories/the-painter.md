# The painter who could not paint the sun

- **Kind:** plain
- **State:** built
- **Someone:** a landscape painter, proud, in a smock stiff with every colour he has thrown away
- **Places:** four abandoned easels along the tracks to the Unfinished Prospect; the Prospect; the painter himself east of it
- **Touches:** nothing yet

## The story

He came to paint this country under its last sun, and the sun would not hold still. He began with one confident crimson line. At the next viewpoint he painted the sun five times, each lower than the last as it sank while he worked, and scraped out four. He painted the keep, quite well, and slashed it corner to corner. He kicked the next one through. The Prospect was to be the great one, and it stands unfinished. He is alive, sitting on his pack with his back squarely to the view and the sun, painting a medlar on a stone with enormous care. Come close and he clutches the little picture to his chest.
