# The copyist

- **Kind:** plain
- **State:** built
- **Someone:** a mason of the Quarrels, set to cut the new house's mouldings
- **Places:** the Quarrels, the brink before the Unroofed House's west end
- **Touches:** [The saint in the kiln](the-saint-in-the-kiln.md) (the same masons, the same keep's stone); the Unroofed House (`new-house`, its pale cut ornament)

## The story

The masons are building a new house out of the keep's own fallen wall, and whoever is paying wants it to have the old mouldings, the egg and the tongue, the way the Heliarchs cut them. One of the masons has a length of the keep's cornice on a block in front of him and a fresh block beside it, and he is copying it. He cannot do it. His eggs come out lumpy and none the same size, one of them has broken off, and two blocks he has already spoiled lie by him on the ground, one on its side. He taps for a while, then sits back and looks from theirs to his and back, and shakes his head, and starts again. The new house's ornament, when a visitor looks at it after, is a little wrong all over.

## What it ties together

Why the new house's ornament reads a little like toys (two readers said so of it): it is a copy by somebody who could not. The Heliarchs' skill is gone; their stone is all that is left to work.
