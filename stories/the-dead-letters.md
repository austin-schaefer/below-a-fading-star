## The story

The Lagan keeps letters that nobody has come for. Its thirty pigeonholes are full of them, sealed in red, and one is stuffed so full it has split: they are all for the same man, a careful traveller in a saffron cloak who said he would call for his letters on his way east and never came. The clerk sorts them, reads the directions again as if they might have changed, and puts them back. When a stranger comes up to the counter he takes one out and holds it across to them, hopefully, and looks at them, and takes it back. The visitor who has rung the bell under the Span knows where the man is.
