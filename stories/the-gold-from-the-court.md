# The gold from the court

- **State:** built
- **Someone:** a jackdaw, a thief with taste
- **Places:** the Sunken Court's west floor; the track from it; a mole heap by the causeway; the Wayside Shrine
- **Touches:** the sign of the sun in its notch (the Heliarchy's hand, `briefs/v8/index.md`); the moles (v7)

## The story

The Heliarchy set the sun in gold in the floor of their court. A jackdaw has been picking the gold out of it, tessera by tessera, the western half of the disc first, and carrying it along the track. It drops some. It keeps the rest in its nest, which it has built on the head of the saint in the Wayside Shrine, so that the saint wears a crown of sticks and stolen gold. The jackdaw has left one tessera in the offering bowl.
