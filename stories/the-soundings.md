# The soundings

- **Kind:** deep, with a plain surface: a great crystal hangs out of the sky on a thread, its point stuck fast in the ice
- **State:** built (v12-H2)
- **Someone:** the leadsmen, whoever is up there at the top of the thread, sounding the world as a sailor sounds a sea. Never seen; their line and their leads are all that come down
- **Places:** the Plummet at the head of the glacier; the old leads frozen into the icefall's steps; the bridge on R2; the broken eye on the shelf at the lip of the last crevasse; the mother crystal at the Lattice
- **Touches:** [The Reckoner under the door](the-reckoner.md) (the eye's band carries his lintel's eleven glyphs; the mother crystal is the oldest lead); the thread and its climbing mote (`src/skylife/remnants.js`)

## The story

Somewhere above the dark there are people who want to know how deep it is: how far below them the floor of the world has sunk since the sun began to fail. So they sound it, as a leadsman sounds a sea: a lead let down on a line until it touches bottom. Their leads are crystal, six-sided and long, with a point at the bottom and a bronze collar and eye at the top for the line; the one hanging now is a hundred and eighty metres from its point to its eye.

Each lead comes down over the head of the glacier, and the ice takes it. The point goes in, the ice closes round it, and the lead stays. In time the line parts, and the leadsmen let down another. The glacier carries each old lead down the notch, slowly, and turns it over as it goes: under the shelf one shows its point out of a cliff of ice, turned half over; lower, one lies sideways with its collar out of the face; one has come to rest across a crevasse, and people walk up it. The oldest the ice let go long ago at its snout, upright on the moor: the mother crystal. The Lattice grew from it, and the line of crystals that climbs the icefall is the glass the leads put out into the ice, going back up to the one that still hangs.

The lead's eye that lies on the shelf broke off one of the old leads. Its band is cast with eleven glyphs, the same eleven the Reckoner keeps on his lintel under the Hypogeum, in the same order: the leadsmen keep his count. The frayed end of its line is still knotted through the eye and still faintly lit, as the thread is. A mote of light climbs the thread from the Plummet's eye into the dark every few minutes: the reading going up.

Somebody came up once from the valley and cut a stair in the ice below the shelf to reach it. The ice has tilted the stair since and broken it in the middle. Nobody knows who.

## What it ties together

The mother crystal (which had no history but its size) with the thread in the sky (which rose from nowhere), the glacier (which was a ramp), and the Reckoner's count (which now somebody else keeps too). The gutter is the one shared event: the Reckoner counts it from below, the leadsmen sound for it from above.

## What the visitor never learns

Who the leadsmen are, how far up they are, what depth they read, and why they count the gutters too.

## Open threads

- Who cut the stair? The Late folk climb for nothing; the Heliarchy's people cut stone, not ice. Perhaps the astronomer, who walks the world without a story.
- A lead's line parts; where does the rest of it fall? A length of glowing line found somewhere on the moor would be a beat.
- The Reckoner turns his row at every gutter. Does the Plummet do anything at a gutter (a shudder in its line, the mote going up early)?
