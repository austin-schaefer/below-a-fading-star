## The story

The carrier that fell off the end of the daylight main was a vessel for noon, and a man lives in its sound half now. He is sure there is still noon in it, somewhere in the hull below the door, a seam of it the Noonwrights never drew off, and he means to find it before anyone else thinks of it. All day he stands with one ear flat to the hull and taps it with a small brass hammer and listens. If you come near he lifts his hand from the hull and puts a finger to his lips until you have gone by.
