## The story

The pedlars carry salt from the flats to the house. A sack has split on the road and nobody stopped for it. The Twk-men, who will tell you anything for a pinch of salt, have found more salt than they have ever been paid and are ferrying it away a grain at a time, in a line, squabbling. When you come near, the line stops dead and every rider watches you until you pass.

## The turn (v10, an act: written, not built; `?salt=1`)

At the split sack the visitor can take up a pinch of the spilt salt; at the guard's pile outside the Pomegranate House's gate they can pay it. Grains are thrown onto the pile; two of the guard's riders come in from out on the turf, take a grain each and go off fast and high toward a far place, and the guard lets fall the payment, news as a thing on the tile: first a scrap of saffron cloth, cream-lined, with a madder border (the traveller on the Span: the pedlar looked up at the Span, saw the man go over, and dropped his sack; the riders go off toward it), then a strip of faded ember with a tuft of black wool (the twelfth chair's coat; toward the Collation), then two gold tesserae on a leaf (the jackdaw; toward the Wayside Shrine). Three payments, each kept. `src/v10/salt.js`.
