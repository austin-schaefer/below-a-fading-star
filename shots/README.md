# Screenshots

The curated record of how the wilderness was built, one flat folder. About 80 images, chosen so the story reads in order: the first barren renders, the Mars colour era and the olive grade that replaced it, the derpy humans before their rework, the deodand that set the standard, before and after pairs from the refinement pass, each landmark and creature at its best.

Agents write raw work-in-progress renders into `wip/` (the inbox). At each merge the orchestrator curates `wip/` into this folder and deletes the rest; git history keeps every original.

## Naming

`<stage NN>-<subject>-<view>[-before|-after].<png|jpg>`: lowercase, hyphens, the subject word first so images of the same thing sort together (`05-structures-keep-gate`, `05-structures-keep-donjon`, `05-structures-tower`). `-before` and `-after` mark a decision pair. `.jpg` files are the agents' original JPEG renders; nothing was converted.

## Stages

| NN | What happened |
|----|---------------|
| 00 | Core: terrain, sky, fog, the sun and the glacier |
| 01 | Rocks |
| 02 | Water and particles: Lees Tarn, the fen and its corpse-candles |
| 03 | Fauna and audio: the deodand, the herd, waders and fliers |
| 04 | Colour shift: the red "Mars" grade replaced by olive and ochre |
| 05 | Structures: the keep, tower, taverna, stones, span, court |
| 06 | Vegetation: grass, reeds, wood, orchard |
| 07 | Full scene: everything together |
| 08 | Sky life: meteors, the great lattice, fliers |
| 09 | Uncanny fauna: the traveller, the cloak of eyes, the slinker, the woman in the tarn |
| 10 | Refinement pass: before and after fixes (fog, sun, reeds, cloak) |
| 11 | Controls: phone thumbstick and debug panel |
| 12 | River and the Speculum |
| 13 | v2 stage C: ruins, flora, river life, sky, inhabitants, more life |
| 14 | v3 stage 1: the loading bar, the profiler and the bench |
| 15 | v3 stage 2: the cheap wins (bloom at half size, pixel ratio by tier, the rejected hard PCF, instanced trees on Firefox) |
| 16 | The flickering spars: seed heads and florets that floated off their stems, and the fix |
| 17 | v3 stage 3: the medium tier (before = high, after = medium) and the lean canvas |
| 18 | The v4 master placement plan: every site for v4, v5 and v6 on the map, and the regions alone |
| 19 | v3 stage 3: vegetation and materials (terrain bands, far grass and reed LOD, the water band, the chunk scan, grass by tier) |

`plan-v2-placement-map.png` is the v2 placement plan, unnumbered.
