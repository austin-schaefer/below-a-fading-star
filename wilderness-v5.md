# Wilderness v5: diversity, life and detail

> **Sequencing (2026-11-08):** the three releases below became layers across versions instead: the land (bioregions, tarn, glacier, Tinctures, gutter, terrain set-dressing) ships in **v4** with v4's own terrain work; the built things in **v5**; the people and small life in **v7**.

Austin, 2026-11-08, after walking v2.1. Runs after v4. Austin picked from `IDEAS-v5.md` on 2026-11-08 (below); the placement plan follows when v5's turn comes.

"If v4 is about improving high-level map nuance, v5 should be about more diversity, more little characters hiding out there, a few more humanoids in particular." Ideas are brainstormed in `IDEAS-v5.md`; Austin picks.

"More variation around the map. The same stones and plants occur everywhere. It would be more powerful if we had more distinct bioregions. The forest, the fens and the blue crystal area do this well. Need more sense that areas have personality: their own flora, fauna, even geology."

## The brief

- The region today has three places with a real character of their own: the Deodand Wood, Corpse-Candle Fen and the Lattice. Everything else shares one palette of grass, heather, rocks and fauna, so the moor between the landmarks reads as the same moor.
- v5 divides the bowl into named bioregions, each with its own ground cover and flowers, its own stone (colour, shape, how it lies: scree, pavement, tors, boulders), its own trees or absence of them, its own small life and sounds, and its own weather in the air (dust, seed, mist, ash). A visitor should know which region they are in with the HUD off, and crossing a boundary should feel like something.
- Candidates to grow from what exists: the orchard country round Medlar Close; the salt and grey reeds of the Wrong Colour spreading into a real salt-flat; the glacier's foreland and moraine; the high heath under the eastern wall; the river's canyon; the keep's home moor. Each gets a one-line character and a palette before anything is built.
- The rule from v2 holds: creatures tied to places. Each bioregion gets at least one thing that lives only there.
- **Light and illumination** (Austin): v4 raises the floor and adds a few local lights; v5 treats light as part of a place's character and as life: each bioregion's own quality of light and afterglow, lights that are tended or carried by someone, lights that move or answer, and the shadows they throw.

### The sun-gutter lasts longer

Austin, on reading the picks: "these are making me realise how many things depend on sun-guttering. The gutter may need to last a bit longer." Today (`sky.js`): a gutter runs 6.5 to 9 s in all, of which about three to five are at the dim floor, with 1.4 s down and 2.5 s back; they come every 70 to 150 s (the first at 25 to 55 s). v5 lengthens it: 12 to 16 s in all, about eight at the floor, every 60 to 110 s, so that the lamps, the beetles, the peafowl, the toadstone and the river's glow have time to be seen and the Leerie's row stays lit long enough to walk under. Everything keyed to the gutter reads `uFlicker` or the `gutter` event, so the one change reaches them all.

### Too much for one version

Austin: "really excellent stuff. I do think this might be too much for a single version?" It is, so v5 ships in three numbered releases, each curated and merged on its own, and the plan stops being a plan at whatever point the night ends:

1. **v5.0, the land:** the eight bioregions with their one creature each, the tarn's new shore, the glacier rebuilt, the Tinctures, the longer gutter, and the cheap set-dressing that belongs to a region (the Collation, the Discontinued Track, the Slipper, the Long Figure, the Surveyor's Pegs, the Unfinished Prospect if doable).
2. **v5.1, the people:** the nine humanoids, the Link-Boy and the Kept Fire.
3. **v5.2, the small life and the light:** the six little characters, the glyph motif, the Luminous Reach.

If Austin wakes between releases, what has shipped is the version and the rest is the next.

**Idea:** every several minutes a band of shadow crosses the bowl 200 m up, west to east at a walking pace, as if something vast were passing between the bowl and the sun. Nothing is drawn: look up and there is only haze, and the sun's disc dims along one edge. Birds under it go quiet.

The first pass comes 40–90 s after the frames start, then 180–420 s after each one ends. 2 m/s is the top of the walking-pace range, and six minutes still reads as one event: the band is in the air over the west for the first three minutes, then climbs the east wall for the last three, and the hush moves with it the whole way.
- **Derived from the sun:** nothing is hand-placed. A point is in the band's shadow when its ray toward the sun (`SUN.dir`, 13.5° up) passes through the body. **The walls:** the terrain's fragment shader (`terrain.js` calls `injectOccultation` after `injectAnnals`, after `lights_fragment_end`) intersects that ray with the body (an inner and an outer box, averaged, soft over the last 40 m of the ray's run through it) and takes 80% of the direct light (the sun is the only directional light) and 30% of the indirect light. It applies to fragments from the bench's brow up (faded over 108–122 m) and inside r 500. A low sun puts the shadow of a 60 m body about 75 m tall on a steep face, and it climbs the wall as the body nears it: on the Annals' band and the Gradine's wall about 170–200 s in, at the crest by about 330 s. **The floor:** the shadow of a body 200 m up lands 4.2 m east for every metre it falls, so on the floor it would land about 830 m east, past the east wall. The clamp loses nothing true. Under the saddle, where the sun is lowest over the ground, the body's shadow falls east onto the bowl's floor and is clamped away, and the saddle's flanks face away from the sun. **The air:** a shared chunk in the fog (`atmosphere.js` calls `injectOccultFog`, which reaches every fogged material: the built-in ones through `ShaderLib`, the ShaderMaterials (water, river, pool, particles, paths and the rest) through `UniformsLib.fog`) measures how far the camera's ray to the fragment runs through the body itself (the same soft boxes) and takes up to 50% of the haze and the low mist along it. It also takes 45% of the haze in front of a shadowed wall fragment, because the air at a shadowed wall is shadowed too. The terrain hands its wall term to the fog through a global. The sky dome (`sky.js` calls `injectOccultSky`) takes the same air term to infinity at up to 45%, so the band shows crossing the bowl where no wall stands behind it: a darker stretch of the glow and the haze, never an edge drawn against the sky.
- **The disc's bite:** from the bowl the line to the sun reaches 200 m only 750 m west, past the saddle. The thing is therefore always above the sun's line, and the bite is on the disc's upper limb, its eastern one. It is a soft chord, 22% darker, whose inner edge starts 0.15 radii above the disc's middle as the pass begins (envelope rising over 25 s). The edge recedes up and off the limb as the band goes east, and the disc is clean by the time the band reaches the east wall. The seething rim on that limb dims with it.
- **The hush:** `ctx.hush = { level(x, z), cue(name, pos) }`. `level` is the pass's envelope within the body's ground footprint (straight below it) plus 100 m, soft over 20 m, and 0 beyond. `audio.js` asks `ctx.hush.cue` first thing in `ctx.audio.cue` (one line), so a bird's cue (`crows`, `rooks-lift`, `flush`, `stonechat`, `grouse-flush`, `wader-gone`) is dropped with probability 0.97 × level at its position, or at the walker's position when it has none. `audio.js` also hands its region kit `hush(pos)`, and the Demesne's rooks scale their gain by 1 − hush (one line in `v4/regions/demesne.js`); they are the one bird voice that is a bed rather than a cue. The sandpipers have no call. Nothing sounds: the idea says the birds go quiet, and a pressure cue would be a thing to hear, so the hush is the whole of it.
- **Cost:** one `vec4` uniform, `uOccult` (envelope, the body's middle x and z, its half-thickness). Every program shares the same plain object: the fog's chunk adds it to every built-in `ShaderLib` entry that carries the fog's uniforms, and `cloneUniforms` copies a value without `isVector4` by reference. It is written once a frame, with no draw calls and no allocation. With no pass running the envelope is 0, and every chunk is one compare per fragment. During a pass, a wall fragment 108–230 m up adds two ray–box intersections (about 40 ALU instructions), every fogged fragment two more, and the dome two. ### DP. The Luminous Reach (`src/v7/reach.js`; `?reach=0`, `?reach=mask`)

"Where the river breaks white in the Clough the water flashes a faint blue-green, plankton lit by the churn, and the glow is carried downstream on the current, out of the canyon and across the slow reach into the fen, fading as it goes. During a gutter it is the brightest line on the bowl's floor, a thread from the fall to the fen." Austin: "Very yes."

Built as two chunks in shaders that already exist: the river ribbon's (`water/river.js` calls `injectReachRiver`) and the tarn's and fen's shared disc (`water.js` calls `injectReachFen`; the chunk runs only where `uFen`, so the tarn draws as before). No draw call, no light, no program of its own; `?reach=0` leaves both hosts' shaders exactly as they were.

- **Sources:** wherever the ribbon runs faster than 1.6 m/s (the weight ramps to full at 3.4 m/s), wherever it is rough (the rapids at s 30–34 and 54–58, the roughness `river.js` gives the ribbon), and the Speculum's churn where the pool spills into the ribbon (s 2.5–7.5).
- **The carry:** a glow profile over the river's arc length, built once into a 256×1 `DataTexture`. Each metre of source excites the water toward saturation (0.25 per metre); the light decays with travel time (ds / speed) over 70 s. The profile is 0 above s 2.9, near 0.9 through the canyon, 0.885 at the last source (s 93.8, the end of the slow reach) and 0.54 at the ribbon's end. It is drawn as soft streaks stretched along the flow and carried at the current's speed, strongest mid-stream, so the glow is seen to travel. In the white water a fine sparkle of points twinkles at 6–10 Hz.
- **Into the fen:** from the mouth (where the ribbon gives itself to the fen's disc) a plume along the current's line, half-width 4.5 m widening 0.32 m per metre, e-folding over 35 m and gone by 57 m. The duckweed and pads cover it.
- **Colour:** `#5cd8e4`, hue about 186: between the wisps (`#9fe8c2`, about 150) and the crystal (`#7ad7ff`, about 200), clear of the moss (`#46e8c8`, about 168).

In daylight the thread adds 0.04 (linear) to near-black water and the sparkle at most 0.32 in the white water, below the bloom threshold (0.88). The glow is added after the fog with the haze dimming it (35% plus 65% of the haze's transmittance), as the lamps' and wisps' sprites are, so it carries further than the water's own colour.
- **Sightlines:** from the rim (`v7-06-canyon-reach-rim-day/-gutter`) the canyon's water turns from faintly green to a carried blue-green; from a height south-west of the river (`v7-06-canyon-reach-bowl-gutter`, 170 m up at (89, 358)) it is a blue-green thread from the fall's foot to the Span's bay, brighter than the fen's wisps and the Leerie's row in the same frame; the Lattice's crystals are brighter, but are not a line; the moss and the Kept Fire are behind that view.
