# Wilderness v5: diversity, life and detail

Austin, 2026-11-08, after walking v2.1. Runs after v4. Initial scope only; the plan comes later.

"If v4 is about improving high-level map nuance, v5 should be about more diversity, more little characters hiding out there, a few more humanoids in particular." Ideas are brainstormed in `IDEAS-v5.md`; Austin picks.

"More variation around the map. The same stones and plants occur everywhere. It would be more powerful if we had more distinct bioregions. The forest, the fens and the blue crystal area do this well. Need more sense that areas have personality: their own flora, fauna, even geology."

## The brief

- The region today has three places with a real character of their own: the Deodand Wood, Corpse-Candle Fen and the Lattice. Everything else shares one palette of grass, heather, rocks and fauna, so the moor between the landmarks reads as the same moor.
- v5 divides the bowl into named bioregions, each with its own ground cover and flowers, its own stone (colour, shape, how it lies: scree, pavement, tors, boulders), its own trees or absence of them, its own small life and sounds, and its own weather in the air (dust, seed, mist, ash). A visitor should know which region they are in with the HUD off, and crossing a boundary should feel like something.
- Candidates to grow from what exists: the orchard country round Medlar Close; the salt and grey reeds of the Wrong Colour spreading into a real salt-flat; the glacier's foreland and moraine; the high heath under the eastern wall; the river's canyon; the keep's home moor. Each gets a one-line character and a palette before anything is built.
- The rule from v2 holds: creatures tied to places. Each bioregion gets at least one thing that lives only there.

## Not in v5

Performance is v3; the wonders, the light and the paths between places are v4. v5 is the land between them.
